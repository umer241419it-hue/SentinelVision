# SentinelVision — Known Issues & Test Artifacts

## Stage 5 Chaincode Schema Validation Test Artifacts

During the Stage 5 Part B deployment of chaincode definition `basic` v1.2 (Sequence 2) on `mychannel`, manual test invocations were executed to validate 9-field schema acceptance and rejection rules. Two specific artifacts relate to this validation:

### 1. `finding-stage5-test9` (Schema Validation Artifact in World State)
- **Asset ID**: `finding-stage5-test9`
- **Module Name**: `InferenceProvenance-Ed25519Seal`
- **Stored Signature**: `"TEST_SIGNATURE_PLACEHOLDER"`
- **Read-Time Status**: `signatureStatus: "TAMPERED"`
- **Explanation**: This record was submitted via CLI during Step 6 of Part B to prove that the newly deployed chaincode accepts 9 parameters and stores the `signature` attribute in world state. Because the signature provided was an explicit placeholder string (`"TEST_SIGNATURE_PLACEHOLDER"`) rather than a genuine 64-byte Ed25519 signature generated by `InferenceProvenance`'s private key, the read-time verification logic in `bridge/src/index.js` correctly flags this finding as `TAMPERED`.
- **Operational Impact**: This is an expected schema-validation test artifact, **not** a production finding or an authentic security compromise. It should be disregarded during demo queries.

### 2. `finding-stage5-reject8` (Rejected Schema Invalidation Attempt)
- **Asset ID**: `finding-stage5-reject8`
- **Submission Attempt**: 8-argument invoke without signature
- **Ledger Status**: **Not Present in Ledger World State**
- **Explanation**: During Step 6 of Part B, an 8-parameter submission was attempted to verify chaincode-level presence enforcement. Peer endorsement failed with `status: 500` (`"Expected 9 parameters, but 8 have been supplied"`). The transaction was rejected before reaching the orderer and was never committed to the ledger world state.

### 3. Pre-Upgrade Legacy Findings (`UNSIGNED_LEGACY`)
- **Asset IDs**: `finding-clean-01`, `seal-12998250-3384-4f66-a0db-080eb5dd5f39`
- **Read-Time Status**: `signatureStatus: "UNSIGNED_LEGACY"`
- **Explanation**: These records were committed under the original Stage 2/Stage 4 8-field chaincode schema prior to the Stage 5 Part B signature retrofit. They do not contain a `"signature"` field. The Fabric bridge returns `HTTP 200 OK` with `signatureStatus: "UNSIGNED_LEGACY"`. They are protected by Fabric's multi-organization endorsement policy at commit time, but lack cryptographic signature binding on read.
